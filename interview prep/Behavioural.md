Here are common behavioural interview questions for Software Engineering Management roles:

## Leadership

- Tell me about a time you led a team through ambiguity.
	- M&S - Building the initial GraphQL operation for mobile pdp. Collected goals and requirements, shopped for stakeholders, presented a draft PRD to the team and got consensus on what to build. Ultimately, it was the wrong thing, shifting priorities ended up pushing us in another direction, but the original thing that was built was still used by the web teams.
    
- How have you handled a situation where your team was behind schedule?
	- Gatsby - We were constantly behind schedule. Tried moving the deadline - not budging. Tried reducing the scope - not happening. Asked the team to work overtime - everyone agreed that it was necessary to get the project done. Higher levels of leadership than myself decided to institute a PTO ban for employees on the project. We leaned heavily on AI, cutback on quality controls, eased standards around code review (after discussion with project/program leadership), increased the accepted amount of technical debt for the project. 
    
- Describe a time you had to make a difficult trade-off as a manager.
    
- Tell me about a time you set a vision or direction for a team.
    

## People Management

- How do you coach underperforming engineers?
    
- Tell me about a time you gave difficult feedback.
    
- Describe a time you supported someone’s growth or promotion.
    
- How do you handle conflict between team members?
    

## Execution and Delivery

- Tell me about a time you delivered a project with limited resources.
    
- Describe a situation where you had to recover from a missed deadline or failure.
    
- How do you prioritize competing demands from stakeholders?
    
- Tell me about a time you improved team execution or productivity.
    

## Technical Judgment

- Tell me about a time you made a technical decision with incomplete information.
    
- Describe a time you disagreed with an architectural approach.
	- Proposed event-driven architecture and the client wanted a simpler N-layer architecture, which would have worked just fine, no problem for the MVP of the application. But it would not have met the scaling requirements being outlined by the business. 
    
- How do you balance speed and quality?
    
- Tell me about a time you influenced a technical decision without direct authority.
    

## Stakeholder Management

- Tell me about a time you managed expectations with product or executive leadership.
    
- Describe a time you had to communicate bad news upward.
    
- How do you handle conflicting priorities across teams?
    
- Tell me about a time you influenced people outside your team.
    
## Hiring and Team Building

- What do you look for when hiring engineers?
    
- Tell me about a time you improved your hiring process.
	- Created several interview formats for new roles - data, cloud, react native
	- Oversaw the creation of the take home test for Java developers
	- Revamped the core interview for web and mobile devs to build a feature on the spot during an on-site. 
    
- Describe a time you onboarded a new engineer successfully.
	- This works a bit differently in consulting where I do not work directly with my direct reports.
    
- How do you build a healthy team culture?
	- Trust and Empathy
	- Built through understanding, listening, and transparency. If I can't get some sort of result for someone I am clear that I cannot guarantee their favoured outcome, but I can promise them I will have answers for them.

- Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
    
- Tell me about a time you dealt with a difficult teammate or stakeholder.
    
- Tell me about a time you handled a production incident.
    
- Tell me about a time you had to adapt to major change.
