### Customer success playbooks: How to build one + free templates
### 5.
Assign team member roles
Assigning team member roles is pivotal to ensuring a cohesive and effective strategy.
Begin by assessing each team member’s strengths, expertise, and experiences.
Align these customer service skills with the specific tasks required, such as:
- Content creation.
- Data analysis.
- Customer engagement.
- Strategy formulation.
For instance, a team member with excellent communication skills could handle customer feedback sessions.
**Clearly define each role’s responsibilities and expectations to prevent overlapping workflows and ensure that all aspects of the playbook are covered.** Regular check-ins and collaborative sessions ensure everyone is aligned, fostering a unified approach that capitalizes on each team member’s strengths.

### Best Practices for a Technical Support team - Upspir
## 5.
Have regular training sessions for your technical support teams
In **technical support roles**, training is much more important than any other aspect.
It is often more crucial than knowledge itself.
This is why all new employees must go through training sessions and assessments at the end of the sessions.
While many companies have this in place, you can excel by conducting refresher training regularly.
These sessions should be especially useful when a new product is launched or after a software update.
This helps your staff stay acquainted with the latest tech advancements and ensures they are not lagging behind technologically.

### The Rise of Technical Support Engineers - Jam
## A practical roadmap to upskill your support team
Transforming a traditional support team into a technical support engineering organization doesn&#39;t happen overnight.
It requires deliberate investment, structured learning, and patience as team members develop new capabilities.
### 1.
Skills audit and gap analysis
Start by honestly assessing your current team&#39;s technical capabilities against the competencies required for your product and customer base.
Which team members already have scripting experience?
Who&#39;s comfortable with SQL?
Where are the biggest skill gaps?
This audit informs hiring decisions (hire experienced technical support engineers or upskill current team members?) and training priorities (which skills deliver the most immediate impact?).
> [!cite]- Source · *Dec 17, 2025*> [The Rise of Technical Support Engineers - Jam](https://jam.dev/blog/the-rise-of-technical-support-engineers/)

### From Good to Great: 7 Best Practices in Technical Support - Transec
## 2.
Train your technical support team well.
Training your tech support team is more than just a checkbox on a to-do list.
It’s laying the groundwork for success.
Start with gathering the training essentials like a well-organized knowledge base, help center, product manuals, scripts, step-by-step guides, and the like.
It’s also good to jazz up your training sessions with visuals and interactive elements to keep things interesting and help your team remember what they learn.
