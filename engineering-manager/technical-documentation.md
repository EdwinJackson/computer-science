## Definition & Scope

> *What is this concept, and what does it include or exclude?*

The right document management system can transform how engineers store, access, and manage documents and data.
Implementation success hinges on a strategy that is both easy to follow and secure.
Here are the must-follow best practices.
1. Perform an assessment of current document systems
2. Prioritize security and access control
3. Ensure integration with existing systems
4. Regular training and skill upgradation
5. Maintain regular backups and establish a document lifecycle

## Best Practices for Engineering Document Management
##### 1.
Use a Robust Document Identification System
Every engineering document should have a unique document number, revision/version number, and clear description.
Include attributes like project number, package number, area code, and document type for easy searchability.
##### 2.
Assign Scheduled Dates
Attach target dates for every stage from creation to review to approval to keep the team aligned and accountable.
This ensures documents move efficiently through the workflow.

### Complete Guide to Technical Documentation Best Practices - Paligo
### Define Collaborative Workflows and Review Processes
Technical documentation is rarely written by a single person.
You need collaborative input from developers, feedback from customer support, and approval from product managers.
Define who does what and when.
Who writes the first draft?
Who reviews for technical accuracy?
Who checks for clarity?
Who gives final approval?
Build review cycles into your product development process.
When you ship a new feature, updating the documentation isn’t an afterthought – it’s part of shipping the feature.

### 10 Technical Documentation Best Practices for 2025
- **Use Templates to Enforce Standards:** Create templates for common document types like tutorials, API references, or release notes.
This ensures structural consistency and guides contributors to include all necessary information, reducing the cognitive load of starting from a blank page.
- **Establish Review SLAs:** Set clear service-level agreements for documentation reviews, just as you would for code reviews.
For example, a rule could be that all documentation reviews must be completed within 48 hours to prevent bottlenecks in the publishing process.
