## The 5 parts of an executive summary
A strong executive summary generally includes five key sections:
1. **Introduction and problem statement:** State the purpose of the document and the problem it addresses.
2. **Proposed solution:** Outline the recommended solution or project approach with clear specifics.
3. **Value and impact:** Explain the benefits of implementing the solution and how it adds value.
4. **Key findings and analysis:** Summarize the data or research that supports your recommendation.
5. **Conclusion and next steps:** Wrap up with a final summary and outline immediate next steps.
