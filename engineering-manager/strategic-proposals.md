### Effective Communication Tips for Engineering Managers
## 2.
Be Clear and Concise
As a manager, you’ll often need to communicate complex ideas.
Avoid jargon or overly technical language when speaking with non-engineers or stakeholders.
- **Use simple language.** Explain ideas as if you’re talking to someone outside your field.
- **Be concise.** Stick to the main points, avoiding unnecessary tangents.
- **Organize your message.** Use bullet points or numbered lists in written communication.
- **Clarify expectations.** Clearly state action items and deadlines.
