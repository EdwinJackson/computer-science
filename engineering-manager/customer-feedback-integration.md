### Product feedback loop template - Customer Success Collective
1. **Feedback collection** – Gather insights from key sources like support tickets, NPS surveys, EBRs, emails, and in-app messages.
2. **Categorization** – Organize feedback by product area, type (e.g., bug, request), impact, and frequency.
3. **Prioritization framework** – Score feedback using a model like RICE or MoSCoW to guide decision-making.
4. **Feedback tracker** – Keep tabs on the status, ownership, and estimated timelines of each item.
5. **Closing the loop** – Make sure customers hear back on what happened with their feedback (yes, even when it’s a no).
There’s even a bonus section for monthly summaries to help align internal teams on trends, risks, and wins.

1. **Start collecting feedback** across all your customer touchpoints.
2. **Categorize and tag it** to surface themes and trends.
3. **Use a prioritization framework** to determine what to tackle first.
4. **Track progress** with ownership, status updates, and timelines.
5. **Close the loop** by communicating back to customers about decisions made.
